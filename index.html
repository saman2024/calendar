Personal calendars

Start a calendar on any day, such as a birthday, and choose which calendar it counts by. Each of its months begins on the same day of that calendar's month as Day 1, and each new year begins on the anniversary of Day 1 in that calendar. Each calendar is private to you until you share it. Sharing one calendar never shows anyone your other calendars.
